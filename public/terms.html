The service

A native Taiwanese Mandarin teacher reviews the Chinese you send (or writes it from your English description) and returns a natural-sounding version in Traditional Chinese, with short notes where helpful. The free AI check is automatic, may be wrong, and is provided as-is for guidance only.

Plans and delivery

  • Quick Fix: up to 300 Chinese characters, delivered within 24 hours.
  • Polish: up to 1,000 Chinese characters, delivered within 48 hours.
  • Tattoo Check: up to 20 Chinese characters, delivered within 24 hours.
  • Calligraphy design add-on (Tattoo Check only): your checked characters set in 3 Chinese calligraphy typefaces, delivered as a high-resolution image within 48 hours. It is a lettering reference for your artist, not a custom illustration.

Delivery times start when payment is confirmed. Results are sent by email and are also available on your private order page.
