Why Google Translate Chinese sounds wrong (and what to do)

If you’re learning Chinese, dating someone who speaks Mandarin, or doing business in Taiwan, you’ve probably pasted English into Google or ChatGPT and gotten Chinese that is grammatically fine but socially awkward. That’s normal — and fixable.

What machine translation misses

  • Register — lover vs boss vs your partner’s mom need different words.
  • Taiwan vs mainland wording — we specialize in Traditional Chinese as used in Taiwan.
  • Collocations — natives say fixed phrases; translators swap synonyms that natives never use.
  • Length and rhythm — a short tattoo or a LINE message has different rules than an essay.
