Chinese tattoo meaning check — before you ink

Tattoo artists are experts at skin, not necessarily at Mandarin. A wrong character order or a “dictionary phrase” can embarrass you for decades. Here’s what foreigners usually get wrong — and how to verify your text with a real Taiwanese native.

Why “Google Translate Chinese tattoo” fails

Machine translation picks words, not how people actually talk. Common failures:

  • Two nouns with no grammar — e.g. 力量家庭 (“strength family”) reads like a label, not a sentence.
  • Simplified vs Traditional — Taiwan uses Traditional Chinese. Mainland-style characters may look “off” to Taiwanese readers.
  • Positive English, awkward Chinese — “Family is my strength” needs a full clause in Chinese, not three random characters.

What a native check includes

  • Does the meaning match what you intend?
  • Is the word order natural for a native reader?
  • Are there homophones or characters that imply something you didn’t mean?
  • Optional: calligraphy layout image for your artist (add-on).
