You want to sound polite, warm, and culturally aware — not like a textbook. In Taiwan, how you address elders matters as much as grammar.

What sounds wrong to Taiwanese families

  • Over-literal “I love you very much” lines that lovers use, copied into a message to parents.
  • Generic 祝你的父母新年快樂 without titles like 伯父、伯母 or 阿姨、叔叔.
  • Missing a health blessing — 身體健康 is standard in New Year and thank-you notes.
