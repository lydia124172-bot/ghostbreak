    What AI gets wrong

    These are real kinds of mistakes we fix every day. Grammatically "okay" — but no native speaker would say them.

    To a partner

    我愛你非常多

    我真的好愛你

    Word-for-word from "I love you very much". Sounds like a textbook, not a lover.

    To their parents

    祝你的父母有一個快樂的新年

    祝伯父伯母新年快樂、身體健康

    Taiwanese families expect respectful titles and a blessing for good health.

    Tattoo

    力量家庭

    家人是我的力量

    Two nouns stuck together means nothing to a native reader. Forever is a long time.
