Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Native Chinese Check ("we", "us") is a Chinese writing correction service operated by BAFU HOLDINGS at bafuholdings.com. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Your text: the Chinese you paste, your English notes, purpose and tone. We use it only to check and correct your writing.
  • Your email and name: used to send your receipt, your result, and to answer your questions.
  • Payment details: PayPal order and capture IDs and the amount paid. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Technical data: IP address and timestamps, used to prevent abuse (for example, limits on the free AI check) and for support. We use Google Analytics to understand site traffic.

Who sees your text

Only our teacher, who reads it to prepare your correction. If you use the free AI check, your text is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce the automatic feedback. We do not publish your text, and we never use it in examples or marketing without your written permission.

Third-party services

PayPal (payments), Resend (email delivery), Google Gemini (free AI check), Google Analytics (traffic statistics) and Render (hosting). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not send marketing emails unless you ask for them.

Data retention

Orders are kept for support, free revisions and accounting, then deleted on a rolling basis. You can ask us to delete your text at any time after delivery. Just email us your order number.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. Contact us at the address below.
