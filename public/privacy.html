Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

GhostBreak ("we", "our", "us") operates the anonymous breakup email and SMS delivery service at bafuholdings.com. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Your Anonymous Email — used only to send you delivery confirmations and copies. Never shared with the recipient.
  • Recipient contact — email or phone number you provide for delivery. Used only to send the message once.
  • Message content — the breakup letter you generate or edit, plus your chosen sign-off nickname.
  • Payment metadata — PayPal order IDs and payment status. We do not store card numbers.
  • Technical logs — IP, timestamps, and delivery status for fraud prevention and support.

What we do not do

  • We never include your email or phone in the message sent to your ex.
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties.
  • We do not make phone calls or enable two-way contact.

Third-party services

We use Resend (email), Twilio (SMS), PayPal (payments), and Google reCAPTCHA (abuse prevention). Each has its own privacy policy governing their processing.

Data retention

Order records are kept for customer support and legal compliance, then deleted on a rolling basis. You may request deletion by contacting support with your order ID.

Your rights

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your data. Contact us at the address on our Terms of Service page.
