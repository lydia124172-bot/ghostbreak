Frequently Asked Questions

How does GhostBreak work?

Fill out the form, generate a free AI preview, choose a paid plan, and pay via PayPal. We deliver your message anonymously by email and/or SMS. You receive exact copies at your Anonymous Email.

Will my ex see my real email or phone?

No. Messages are sent from our official channels only. Your Anonymous Email and personal contact info are never included in the delivery.

Why didn't the email arrive in the inbox?

Gmail often sorts new senders into Promotions or Spam. Search for noreply@bafuholdings.com or mark the message as "not spam" to improve future delivery.

How long can the SMS be?

Full messages support up to ~1,500 characters and may span multiple SMS segments. Twilio trial accounts are limited to a short single-segment message (~70 characters with Chinese). Upgrade Twilio to paid for full-length SMS.

What's included in each plan?
  • Email ($9.99) — Anonymous breakup email + copy to you
  • SMS ($19.99) — Anonymous SMS + copy to you
  • Bundle ($24.99) — Email + SMS (both ex email & phone required) + English Survival Guide PDF
Can my ex reply?

No. This is a one-way closure service. We do not forward replies or enable two-way contact.

How do I get support?

Email support@bafuholdings.com with your PayPal order ID. Check your Anonymous Email (including spam/promotions) for delivery copies first.
